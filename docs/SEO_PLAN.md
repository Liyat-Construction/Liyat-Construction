# SEO plan — Liyat Construction

Started 2026-11-10 with the owner. This is the plan for being found on Google, on Maps and in
AI answers. Work one step at a time, in order, and tick each step in
[`WORKFLOW.md`](../WORKFLOW.md). Questions for Liyat are in the Notion page "Meeting questions
for Liyat" (Build → Client projects).

## Goal

1. Searching **"Liyat"** or **"Liyat Construction"** shows the website, one correct Google Maps
   profile, and a correct AI answer ("water-well drilling and groundwater engineering").
2. Over months: show up for **"water well drilling Ethiopia"**, "borehole drilling Addis Ababa",
   "pumping test Ethiopia", "hydrogeological survey Ethiopia".
3. The scoreboard is Google Search Console (searches, impressions, clicks). No promise of a
   position: competitors have older sites and more links.

**What it means.** Only Gemini reads our site, and it is right. The other three describe a
general building contractor, and two say it is closed or give an old address. There may be a
second, similarly named "Liyat Construction" (a BC-7 building contractor, registered around
Nifas Silk-Lafto); we have no proof, so we do not claim it. Liyat's own papers (Grade One,
licence WWDC/1/227, on About) are the way to tell them apart. The fix is the same everywhere:
"water-well drilling and groundwater engineering", repeated the same way on the site, structured
data, the Business Profile and LinkedIn page. Home and About already carry a fact sentence that
says this, and it states 2019 and May 2022 correctly (the plan's "founded in 2019" would be less
accurate); step 2 needs to check it, not write it.

## Decisions

- **D-180 — Search Console account (owner, 2026-11-10).** Verified with the agency Gmail
  (`digitalaksumitehq@gmail.com`) as a **Domain** property by one DNS TXT record (we own the
  domain). `liyatconstruction@gmail.com` is then added as an **Owner**, so Liyat keeps access
  if the agency ends.
- **D-181 — Google Business Profile (owner, 2026-11-10).** Owned by Liyat, with the agency as
  Manager. One profile only; Liyat deletes the other. Address, hours and category follow his
  answers. A Business Profile has no email field, so the email stays on the website.
- **D-182 — Profiles (owner, 2026-11-10).** Must: Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company
  page. Also: Facebook page, WhatsApp Business. Not now: Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube.
  Every profile uses the same name, descriptor, address, phones, website and logo.
- **D-183 — Access (owner, 2026-11-10).** Liyat chooses (meeting question 15), and stays the owner
  of everything we create either way:
  - **A. Together (agent's recommendation):** a 30-minute sit-down. He adds us as a manager or
    admin and types or approves each code himself; we never need his password. The `info@`
    address on his domain, forwarding to his Gmail, receives sign-up emails; he forwards the
    contact form key.
  - **B. His Gmail login:** shared privately, used for the setup, changed by him afterwards.
    Rules: never in email, a group chat, Notion, `WORKFLOW.md` or any project file; Google may
    send a code to his phone at sign-in, so he keeps it near; delete the message after use.
- **D-184 — Three project pages (owner, 2026-11-10).** Reopens D-74 and D-96 (no project
  pages). Three projects get their own page, are shown on Home and link from Projects. Every
  other project stays a card.
  - **Chosen (owner's pick, agreed):** Ethiopian Airlines, the British Embassy and Chaka House.
    These are the three Liyat already chose for Home, the names (a national airline, an embassy)
    build trust with the target clients, and the three stories differ: a tested yield (40 L/s,
    two 500 m wells), a turnkey job (survey, 250 m well, pump and tank), and the deepest well
    (600 m).
  - **Photos decide each page.** Ethiopian Airlines has 4 sharp photos. The British Embassy and
    Chaka House have one each (1200 × 1600), so they need more from Liyat (meeting question 8).
    **Fallback for a page with too few photos: Ovid at Kings Tower** (3 sharp photos, two 500 m
    wells, turnkey with pump installation); then Arada Luxury Mall (two sharp photos, needs its
    location, C-36). A page is not built with one photo.
  - **Left out, and why:** CREDOKS is the most unusual story, but its photos are small phone
    images (901 × 1600, 1360 × 766) and shoring is not a service Liyat offers. It stays a card
    on Projects. Entoto (water flowing without a pump) has one photo.
